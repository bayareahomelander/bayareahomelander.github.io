Flint

GPT-2 from scratch on hand-written CUDA kernels·C++17, CUDA

A from-scratch GPT-2 training and inference stack with no cuBLAS, cuDNN or PyTorch at runtime, built to train GPT-2 124M on one RTX 2080 Ti. Version 0.1.0 ships its hand-written FP16 tensor-core matrix multiply at 82–108% of cuBLAS (41–52 TFLOPS) across GPT-2’s layer shapes, bit-identical from run to run. Now in progress: a full training step matching PyTorch, with every forward and backward kernel already written and verified against PyTorch on GPT-2 124M.
