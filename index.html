Flint GPT-2 from scratch on hand-written CUDA kernels

Trains GPT-2 (124M) on one consumer GPU and then serves it, with every kernel written by hand: no cuBLAS, cuDNN, or PyTorch at runtime.

Early result: its matrix multiply already runs at over 80% of cuBLAS on every GPT-2 layer shape.
